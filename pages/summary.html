Actions

How this form works & what gets computed automatically
  • Fillable vs. computed. Only Net Principal Due, Total Interest and Total SUC Dues are read-only — they are derived. Everything else (particulars, AGR items, SUC rates, SUC Dues Payable, SUC Dues Paid, Penalty, Interest on Principal, Interest on Penalty) is fillable.
  • Disputed = Combined − Undisputed. Every cell of the Disputed Dues table is computed as SUC Dues as per CCA − Undisputed SUC Dues, column by column and row by row.
  • Formulas. A = Net Principal Due = Payable − Paid. B = Penalty. A1 = Interest on Principal. B1 = Interest on Penalty. Total Interest = A1 + B1. Total SUC Dues = A + B + A1 + B1. The final Net SUC Due Payable (Undisputed) row mirrors the Undisputed Total SUC Dues row.
  • Rounding. Amounts are displayed rounded up to the next higher rupee — no decimals are shown. All calculations use full precision internally.
  • Paste from Excel. Copy any rectangular block from Excel and paste it into the first cell of a table — values fan out across rows and columns. Currency symbols, thousands separators and % signs are stripped automatically.
  • AGR total formatting. When you leave an AGR Total field, its value is rounded up and re-displayed in Indian numbering format (e.g. 34,05,34,008).
  • Adding / deleting columns. Use the Spectrum columns dropdown in the sidebar.
  • Print / PDF. The Print / save PDF button builds a black-and-white print-only sheet and opens the browser print dialog. Tables are kept whole — a table that does not fit on the remaining space moves to the next page instead of splitting.
  • Excel export. Writes the whole form into an .xlsx workbook with live formulas for every total.
  • Persistence. Everything is remembered in your browser. RESET ALL wipes the saved cache and returns a blank form.
